Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock

How to Play

Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock is played by choosing one of five gestures. Each gesture defeats two others and is defeated by two, keeping the game balanced.

Rock crushes Scissors and Lizard
Paper covers Rock and disproves Spock
Scissors cut Paper and decapitate Lizard
Lizard poisons Spock and eats Paper
Spock smashes Scissors and vaporizes Rock

If both players choose the same gesture, the round is a tie and must be replayed until a winner is decided.
